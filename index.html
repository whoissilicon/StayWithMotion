Stay With Motion reads your device's GPS to calculate real-time speed. Nothing is simulated — every number comes from an actual satellite fix.

Signal may take a few seconds outdoors, and won't be reliable indoors — that's a GPS limit, not an app one.

With motion sensor access, it also counts real steps and estimates step-based distance — shown separately from GPS distance, since they're measured two different ways.
